FAQ

Common questions

What is a merchant cash advance?

A merchant cash advance is a lump-sum advance repaid as a small, agreed percentage of your future card sales. It's a fast and flexible alternative to a traditional business loan.

How much can I borrow?

Advances typically range from £5,000 to £500,000. The amount you can access depends on your card turnover and trading history.

How quickly can I get funded?

Most applications are reviewed within one working day. Once approved, funds are usually in your account the same day.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. You'll see exactly what you repay before you sign. One transparent cost, agreed up front.

Will this affect my credit score?

We perform a soft search when you check eligibility — this does not affect your credit score. A full search is only performed if you proceed to a formal application.

Do I need to provide security?

No. UK Merchant Capital advances are unsecured — you don't need to put up your home, premises or equipment.
